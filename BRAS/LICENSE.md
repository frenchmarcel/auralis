# Licence of the BRAS-derived files

The files in this folder that come from the BRAS database are adapted material under the
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence (CC BY-SA 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). They are:

- the room geometries converted from the BRAS SketchUp and OBJ files (`CR*/*.obj`),
- the materials, source and receiver positions and air conditions in the bench manifests
  (`*.json`), and the source directivity tables derived from the BRAS source data,
- the reports and figures that compare simulations with the BRAS measurements.

Source: BRAS, a Benchmark for Room Acoustical Simulation, TU Berlin, published on
DepositOnce (Brinkmann, Aspöck, Ackermann, Lepa, Vorländer and Weinzierl). Changes: geometry
converted and checked against the documented surface areas, material tables reorganised per
octave band, positions copied into manifests. Share adaptations of these files under the same
licence and keep this credit.

The Python code in this folder (the harnesses and scripts) is under the MIT licence of the
repository.
