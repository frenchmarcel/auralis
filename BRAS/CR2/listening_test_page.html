Real or simulated

CR2 seminar room (RWTH Aachen, BRAS database). Responses measured with the FABIAN artificial head are compared with the Auralis simulation of the same positions, with the same head.

Before you start

Put on headphones and sit in a quiet place. The test takes 15 to 20 minutes.

First set the volume to a comfortable level. Then do not change it until the end.

Part 2: find X

This time you know which is which: A is real and B is simulated. X is one of the two, picked at random. Compare them as often as you like, then say whether X is A or B.

If you hear no difference, answer at random anyway. This is expected. After each answer, you can note what sets B (simulated) apart from A (real). This is optional, but it shows what to improve in the simulation.

Compared with A (real), B (simulated) has… Optional. You can pick several. You can keep listening.

Results

What gave it away? Timbre, reverberation, position of the sounds, width, hiss… Even if you answered at random, describe what you heard.
